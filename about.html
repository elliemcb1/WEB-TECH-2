About Jo Smith Photography

Photography has always been my way of connecting with the world — not just through images, but through emotion, atmosphere, and storytelling.

Based in Dundee, Scotland, I specialise in wedding, portrait, and lifestyle photography that captures genuine moments naturally and authentically.

My journey started with a second-hand camera and a passion for exploring Scotland’s landscapes during golden hour. Over time, photography became more than a hobby — it became my career and creative purpose.

I love creating relaxed photography experiences where people can feel comfortable, confident, and truly themselves. Whether I am photographing weddings, family sessions, or commercial projects, I focus on timeless images filled with emotion and personality.

When I am not behind the camera, you will usually find me hiking across Scotland, editing photos in local cafés, or planning my next creative project.
